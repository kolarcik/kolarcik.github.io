Frequently asked questions

How do I sync thoughts between devices?

Mindonia uses your iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account on every device and keep sync turned on in Mindonia's Settings. Your data goes to your private iCloud storage, not to our servers.

How do I share a map with someone?

Open the map and choose Share Map. Enter the email of the other person's Apple Account and choose whether they can edit. Mindonia prepares an email with a link; they open it on their device with Mindonia installed. You can stop sharing at any time; people with read-only access can't change the map.

Which AI models does Mindonia use?

You choose in Settings › AI: on-device models (private, work offline), Apple Intelligence, Ollama on your own computer, or any OpenAI-compatible server with your own key. AI never changes anything without you – every change is shown as a preview first.

Dictation doesn't work.

Check that Mindonia may use the microphone and speech recognition (System Settings › Privacy & Security). Speech is recognized on your device.

How do I connect Mindonia to Claude Code or another assistant?

On Mac, open Settings › MCP, turn the server on and copy the prepared command. The server runs only on your Mac and accepts requests only with your token. The assistant can add and sort thoughts; deleting is only possible in the app.

How do I export or back up my maps?

Export a map to Markdown, OPML, PDF or an image from the share menu (on Mac, File › Export Map). With sync on, your library is also in your iCloud.
