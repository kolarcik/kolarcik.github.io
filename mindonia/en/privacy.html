Privacy Policy

Mindonia

SupportPrivacyČesky

Effective November 16, 2026

In short: Mindonia doesn't collect any of your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your thoughts stay on your devices and in your iCloud. They go elsewhere only when you choose so – for example when you set up a third-party AI server or share a map.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Thoughts, maps, notes and images are stored in the app's database on your device.
  • In your iCloud. With sync turned on, they're stored in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). The developer has no access to it; it's governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Shared maps. When you share a map, the people you invite can see it (and edit it, if you allow). Invitations use the email of their Apple Account; the lookup happens through iCloud and isn't visible to the developer.

Artificial intelligence

  • On-device models run only on your device. A model is downloaded once from Hugging Face; none of your thoughts are sent.
  • Apple Intelligence processes requests on device or in Private Cloud Compute under Apple's rules.
  • Ollama or an OpenAI-compatible server – if you set one up yourself, Mindonia sends it the text of the thoughts the AI works with. How it's handled is up to that provider's terms. Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain.

Dictation and microphone

The microphone is used only while you dictate, and speech is recognized on your device. Recordings are neither stored nor sent.

MCP server on Mac

If you turn it on, it listens only on your Mac (127.0.0.1) and accepts requests with your token. An assistant you connect (such as Claude Code) receives the content it asks for and handles it under its own terms.

What Mindonia doesn't do

  • collect personal data, usage data or diagnostics,
  • show ads or use third-party tracking tools,
  • sell or pass data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete data in the app, by removing the app, and in iCloud under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Mindonia.

Children

Mindonia collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be on this page. Send questions to support@kollarczik.cz.
